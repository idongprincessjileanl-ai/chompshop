Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Metro Manila orders usually arrive within 1–3 business days, and provincial orders within 3–7 business days. You can follow the status anytime under “My Orders.”

What payment methods can I use?

You can pay with GCash, Maya, a debit/credit card, or Cash on Delivery. Every payment is screened by our AI fraud check for your safety.

Can I return or replace an item?

Yes. Defective or incorrect items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Keep your receipt (we also give a QR receipt) and contact support to arrange a replacement.

Is Cash on Delivery available everywhere?

COD is available for most serviceable areas nationwide. If your address is not eligible at checkout, you can still use GCash, Maya or Card.

Do I need an account to order?

Yes — a quick account lets us save your delivery address, keep your cart, and track your orders. Your account also gets a unique UID shown in your profile.

Security & Row-Level Security

How the system protects each user's data, from the website down to the database.

Login & Permissions

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Logging in gives a signed token (JWT) that says who you are and your role: Customer, Rider, Inventory Staff or Admin. Each API route checks the role.

Rate Limiting

Limits per visitor: 10 login tries per 15 minutes, 10 checkouts per minute, 300 requests per minute. Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 wrong passwords.

Row-Level Security

For every request the API tells MySQL who is logged in (@app_user_id). The rls_* views only return that user's rows, and the customer database account cannot read the raw tables.

Logs & Recovery

Every request gets an ID and is logged. Errors go to error_log, actions to audit_log. Daily backups can be restored with one command.

Database Normalization

1NF

Each field stores a single value. Order items and product specs are stored in their own tables.

2NF

Non-key fields depend on the complete key.

3NF

Non-key fields depend on the key, not on other non-key fields. Categories and suppliers have their own tables.
